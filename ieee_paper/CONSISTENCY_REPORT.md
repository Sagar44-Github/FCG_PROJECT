## Explicit evidence limits

1. No physical printer, label, substrate, illuminant, camera, spectrophotometer, or colorimeter measurements were made.
2. No human participants or retail workers were studied. Observer and trigger rates are synthetic model outputs, not human-response data.
3. The sRGB-to-reflectance solution is non-unique. The virtual print transfer is synthetic and unvalidated against physical print spectra.
4. L2/L3 are parameterized three-Gaussian LED spectra fit to nominal target chromaticities, not measured store fixtures. CCT does not uniquely specify an SPD.
5. Blender Cycles output is RGB visualization; the Python spectral model supplies analytical values.
6. The inventory model is an alternative scenario, not Nigam's method. Arrival rates, red fraction, capacities, discount recovery, horizon, and unit mass are assumptions.
7. Emission factors 1.5/2.5/3.5 kg CO2e per kg are illustrative and have no external factor source. Outputs are not an LCA, realized reduction, or SDG indicator.
8. Pilarski and Li full-text PDFs are not in the local handoff. The manuscript uses metadata/abstract-level descriptions and marks unavailable feature comparisons NV. Jullien and the Wadhawan 2024 preprint have local full texts. The Wadhawan final 2025 PDF is absent.
9. The supplied Nigam copy has no verified venue, DOI, or publication date. It is cited as supplied course context only.
10. Docker and cloud deployment are prepared but unverified. No production deployment is claimed.
11. Faculty items are described at their implemented scope: user-space execution needs no custom kernel module; collection pagination and listed query filters exist; sandbox controls are application-level; and container/cloud deployment is configuration only. This manuscript does not claim a completed cloud deployment, OS-level sandbox certification, or human acceptance study.
12. The provided assignment/rubric and team identifiers are prompt-supplied materials. The two ToC images depict a separate medical/quantum project and are not used as scientific content. The assignment's Blender-only sentence conflicts with the specified software stack and the implemented Python/API application; the paper describes the actual system and keeps Blender's role explicit.
