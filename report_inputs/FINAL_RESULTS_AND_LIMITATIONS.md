# Final results and limitations

## Evidence classification

All values below are generated by the current pipeline with `config.json` and seed `20261115`. **Calculated** denotes deterministic output from sourced spectral tables and configured models. **Simulated** denotes seeded random outcomes under explicit assumptions. These are not physical measurements, human-participant findings, store observations, or realized donations/waste/emissions. The pipeline log is `results/phase4_pipeline_run.txt`; software versions and configuration snapshot are in `results/run_metadata.json`.

## Colour-difference results

The 12 conditions are four configured sRGB colours × L1 D65, L2 approximately 4000 K LED model, and L3 approximately 3000 K LED model. CIEDE2000 compares the digital metamer with the virtual-print reflectance under the same illuminant white. Units are ΔE00 (dimensionless colour-difference units). The raw rows are `results/colour_measurements.csv`; summary statistics are `results/delta_e_summary.csv` and `results/statistical_summary.csv`.

## Sustainability sensitivity

For L1, simulated errors and capacity 25/day, mean saved units are 1198.680, corresponding under the assumed 0.8 kg/unit to 958.944 kg food mass. The configured illustrative CO₂e sensitivity gives low 1438.416 kg (95% replicate interval [1372.170,1501.200]), central 2397.360 kg ([2286.950,2502.000]), and high 3356.304 kg ([3201.730,3502.800]). Source: `results/sustainability_analysis.csv`. These are **modeled potential scenario values** using unverified illustrative factors 1.5/2.5/3.5 kg CO₂e per kg; they are not a measured reduction, source-based product footprint, full LCA, SDG impact evaluation, or Nigam score.

## Limitations that must remain explicit

1. No physical label, substrate, ink, printer, illumination, camera, spectrophotometer or colorimeter measurements were taken.
2. No human participants or retail workers were recruited. The observer probability curve/confusion weights are assumptions; the logit is not human-calibrated.
3. RGB-to-reflectance reconstruction is underdetermined and selects a smooth metamer. The virtual print transfer is synthetic and unvalidated against print data.
4. L2/L3 are parameterized LED SPDs matching target chromaticity/CCT, not measured retail spectra. D65 uses the recognized CIE table.
5. Blender Cycles produces RGB renders; it is not the source of spectral ΔE values.
6. Inventory parameters are an alternative scenario and are not presented as Nigam's model. The supplied five-page Nigam PDF is included for context, but its exact donation-capacity inputs and parameter provenance do not provide a sufficiently sourced, reproducible match to this implementation. Its reported Near-Expiry failure is treated as a source-reported motivation, not reproduced output.
7. Emission factors and average mass are illustrative; outputs are not an LCA or realized savings.
8. Four-color confidence intervals describe only four fixed configurations. Monte Carlo intervals reflect the model's randomness/assumptions.
9. Docker build/Compose runtime and cloud deployment are not verified. Physical validation and empirical observer calibration are future work.
